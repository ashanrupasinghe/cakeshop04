Our Chocolate Cake is the ultimate indulgence for any chocolate lover. We use only the finest Valrhona dark chocolate in every layer, creating an intensely rich flavour that is perfectly balanced by the lightness of our hand-whipped sponge.

Each cake is layered generously with our house-made dark chocolate ganache and finished with an outer coat of silky smooth frosting. A final flourish of chocolate curls and a light dusting of premium cocoa powder completes this showstopper.

Available in three sizes to suit every occasion — from an intimate birthday to a large celebration. Custom inscriptions and decorations available on request.

All ingredients are sourced locally where possible. No artificial preservatives, colours or flavours.
