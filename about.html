Our Story

We Bake with Passion.

Welcome to Cake Shop — where every creation is crafted with heart. Born from a deep love of baking and a commitment to quality, we've been bringing joy to New York tables since 2010.

From humble beginnings in a small kitchen to a beloved neighbourhood bakery, our journey has always been guided by one simple belief: the best cakes start with the best ingredients and the most care.

Cakes – Timeless classic sponges for every celebration and every craving.

Pies – Warming, hearty pies baked fresh with seasonal fruit and buttery pastry.

Tarts – Elegant pastry shells filled with cream, custard and the finest fresh fruits.

What We Stand For

Our Values

Everything we do is driven by a few core values that we never compromise on.

🌿

Natural Ingredients

We use only the finest natural, locally sourced ingredients — no artificial flavours, colours or preservatives.

❤️

Made with Love

Every cake is made by hand with genuine care and attention to detail. That love shows in every bite.

✨

Quality First

We never cut corners. From the butter we use to the decorations we place, quality is our standard.

🎨

Creative Designs

Our bakers are artists. Every cake is a canvas for beautiful, personalised edible artwork.

🤝

Community Focused

We support local suppliers and give back to our New York community through every loaf and every layer.

♻️

Sustainable Baking

We minimise waste, use eco-friendly packaging and continuously improve our environmental footprint.
